# Movie App

A sample movie app using React. 

## Details

- Highlight something in your project that you thought was especially interesting or significant to your overall implementation.

Getting the search results by genre to properly work was interesting, it took some thinking back and doing but getting it to display by genre and the counts to be right for the total movies and pagination was the interesting part.

The significant part was having to understand and learn on the fly the GraphQL portion because I haven't used GraphQl since I was experimenting with Gatsby.

- Tell us what you are most pleased or proud of with your implementation.

I am proud of the entire app and being able to get as far as I did, with the knowledge of React that I have and research how the latest version of React is. That said, every last bit of React, and CSS to get it looking and functioning as it does just reinforces within myself I can still code.

- Given more time, what next feature or improvement would you like to add to your project?

A "Skip to Content" link, some more accessibility improvements, styling improvements. A feature to sort movies alphabetically from A-Z and Z-A, sort by genre, sort by rating, sort by published date.
